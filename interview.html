Privacy & Data Usage

Camera & Microphone

Your camera and microphone are only active during the interview session. We use them to track your focus and listen to your answers. No video or audio is recorded, saved, or shared.

Job Description

Your job description is used only to generate relevant interview questions. It is not stored, saved, or shared with anyone.

Your Answers

Your answers are sent to AI for feedback analysis. No one at Interview Coach reviews your answers. Everything stays in your browser session.

Data Storage

No data is stored on our servers. All data is cleared when you close the browser. You can end the interview at any time.

We built Interview Coach to help you succeed — not to track you. Your privacy matters.
